07 / How we work

Seven things we hold ourselves to.

  1. 01We never claim a test we didn't run. Measured means a published log, an instrument and a receipt.
  2. 02We buy every unit we test, at retail, and post the receipt with the address redacted. A pick without an M mark was chosen from manufacturer specs and our model — we have not tested it.
  3. 03No money changes a result, a ranking, a verdict or a model constant.
  4. 04No display ads — not on the planner, the reports, safety pages or money pages.
  5. 05We publish "Don't buy" verdicts, negative results and our model's misses.
  6. 06We correct within 72 hours, or 24 for safety. Every change goes into the Lab notebook.
