Cost per MMBtu isn't the whole decision

Electric resistance and diesel land close together per delivered BTU at typical Midwest prices — diesel only pulls ahead once electricity costs more than roughly 21¢/kWh, closer to California or New England rates than Illinois'. A mini-split beats resistance heat any month it stays above freezing, then loses that edge on the coldest nights. None of that changes the install rule: combustion heat needs a place for exhaust to go and a CO alarm. Electric heat needs a circuit sized for its nameplate, not just its price tag. For the natural gas row, the natural gas page covers sizing, venting and the gas fitter.

Safety scope

Every combustion system on this page needs a CO alarm in the house near the garage door (A code or reference citationInternational Residential Code, 2021 · checked 2026-09-25Report a problem) and, for the classes we recommend, a garage-rated CO monitor while it runs. Electric classes skip the combustion risk entirely but still need the breaker and wire sized off the nameplate, not a guess.
