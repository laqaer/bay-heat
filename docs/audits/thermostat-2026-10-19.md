## Ratings, cautions and product identity checks

- Independently recomputed: 4,000/240 = 16.6667 A; 5,000/240 = 20.8333 A; 7,500/240 = 31.25 A; 22×240 = 5,280 W; 1,500/120 = 12.5 A; 15×120 = 1,800 W; 15×125 = 1,875 W. None independently establishes installed-equipment compatibility.
- Stelpro's SWT2F sheet supports the 22 A/120–277 V class, double-pole off position and published 50–90°F dial. The [installation guide](https://www.stelpro.com/wp-content/uploads/produits/documents/INS/INS_SWT_I_EN.pdf) calls for qualified installation; the model-specific Fahrenheit sheet controls the dial statement.
- Cadet's T522 positive off, T521's different approximately 41°F left stop, copper-only installation and flammable-storage warning were confirmed. The draft does not call its left stop a freeze-hold setting.
- Honeywell's 40–80°F range, approximately 5 ft inside-wall placement guidance and noninductive rating were confirmed. Its placement guidance is attributed to that model; discontinued/retailer-availability statements were removed as unnecessary and not independently verified against the manufacturer.
- C226T US listing states 15 A/1,800 W; its multi-region manufacturer manual shows 1,800 W at 120 V and generic maximum 16 A. The variant ambiguity is explicit. T9's manual gives 125 V, 15 A/1,875 W resistive/general-purpose, separate motor/other-load limits, indoor/dry restrictions, and distinguishes the waterproof probe from the controller.
- Amazon identity checks matched B00KRFKE7C to SWT2F, B07FTVGXGM to T522-W, B0DBPRCBL5 to C226T, and B07Y2FDR4K to T9. These were listing-identity checks, not independent safety listings, current stock guarantees, product testing, or compatibility approvals. No star ratings, review-count claims or hands-on claims were added.
- Freeze-protection language is qualified: a room sensor does not measure every pipe, a minimum dial value is not a guarantee, and power loss/failure must be considered.

## Remaining requirements

A real editor must review the complete corrected article, and a qualified electrical professional must approve its technical claims at the exact revision. One person can fill both roles if suitably qualified. Repository policy already requires licensed review of electrical/gas rule changes and full human review of safety/verdict pages. This audit is documentary verification by an AI and cannot supply those credentials or attest to a review that did not happen.

An administrator must also require `editorial-review` and `check`, enforce review of governance files and restrict direct pushes/bypass. Main was reported unprotected, repository rulesets were empty, and branch-protection access returned 403. The build gate is enforceable in the code path, but administrators could remove it; do not claim provider-level merge protection is configured. See [review procedure](../editorial/README.md).
