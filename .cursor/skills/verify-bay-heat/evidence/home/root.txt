What circuit do you actually have? 120V vs 240V garage heaters: circuit and breaker reality Voltage is not a feature. It is the limit of the circuit you already have — or the one you are willing to install. -> /120v-vs-240v-garage-heater
Temporary plug-in, or a permanent install? Hardwired vs plug-in electric garage heaters A 15 A cord is Saturday heat at a bench. Whole-bay heat is almost always a dedicated 240 V hardwire. Corded 240 V is a third class — only if the manual lists the plug. -> /hardwired-vs-plugin-garage-heater
Whole bay, or just the person at the bench? Forced-air vs infrared garage heaters Forced-air heats the air. Infrared heats people and objects. Drafts punish the first; open-door work favors the second. -> /forced-air-vs-infrared-garage-heater
Can you hardwire 240 V and hang a 25–30 lb unit from joists? Ceiling-mount garage heaters under $200 Most sub-$200 ceiling units are the same job: 5 kW, 240 V, hardwired, 30 A, 10 AWG copper. The differences are controls and jumpers, not magic BTUs. -> /best-ceiling-mount-garage-heaters-under-200
Stuck with a regular 120 V outlet? Portable garage heaters on a 15 A circuit On a 15 A circuit, treat ~12 A as the continuous ceiling. A 1,500 W milkhouse heater is 12.5 A — it wants to be the only load. -> /portable-garage-heaters-15a-circuit
Can the ceiling take the unit, or is the heat needed on one wall? Wall-mount vs ceiling-mount garage heaters Ceiling units throw across the bay if the joists can take 25–30 lb. Wall mounts win on low ceilings and one work zone. The manual’s clearance is the rule, not a blog height. -> /wall-mount-vs-ceiling-garage-heater
Need heat on one wall, not thrown across the bay? Best wall-mount electric garage heaters Wall-mount is a bracket and a throw, not a new wattage class. The 5 kW FUH can hang on a wall. A 7.5 kW shop unit needs a larger circuit. A 1500 W wall infrared is still a 15 A load. -> /best-wall-mount-garage-heaters
Is the building leaking the heat you already paid for? Insulate the garage before a heater upgrade More watts heat the air you are already leaking. Door, weatherstrip, and sill usually beat a larger breaker. Panel capacity is the hard stop. -> /insulate-garage-before-heater-upgrade
What will the watts actually cost to run? How much an electric garage heater costs to run Cost is nameplate watts, hours the element is actually on, and the rate on your bill. A 1,500 W portable is cheap per hour because it is spot heat, not because 120 V is efficient. -> /electric-garage-heater-operating-cost
Is the bay sealed enough for electric, or is this a vented propane shop problem? Electric vs propane garage heaters Electric wins on enclosed, occupied-adjacent air. Propane can win BTU and $/BTU in a drafty detached shop if it is listed and vented for that job. -> /electric-vs-propane-garage-heater
